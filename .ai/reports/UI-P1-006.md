  - has width equal to `SemanticWidthPx(Preview)`
  - is thicker than 0 and thinner than `SemanticWidthPx(Default)`
  - is dashed or has alpha below 1
  - has the RGB of `SemanticColor(Preview)`

  It restores the original display settings afterwards. It reads the pen through the accessor rather than inspecting paint output, as the revision asked.
- **Leftovers:** no `palette_.preview` pens remain outside `SemanticColor`.

# UI-P1-006 self-review, Stage 1/1 (STAGED, EXTRA_HIGH, no edits made)

## Against the task and spec §3
- **Normal, Hover, Selected, Snap and Preview are separate states.**
  - The state logic lives in core: `SemanticState.h`/`.cpp`, with no Qt or OCCT.
  - The colours come from one place: `V2Viewport::SemanticColor` / `SemanticWidthPx`, which read the theme palette.
  - The line width order is Preview < normal < Hover < Selected at every line width setting (0.25, 0.5, default, 6, 12). The self-test checks this.
- **Only the selected segment of a wire is highlighted.**
  - `RefCoversCurve` is one shared rule. `IsCurveSelected`, `SelectedCurves`, `CurveSemanticState` and `ControlPointsForSelection` all use it, so line colour, grab handles and command targets can't disagree.
  - Selecting the whole object still highlights every segment. Both cases are tested in core and in the self-test.
- **States stay distinct after a theme change.** `SemanticInksAreDistinct` (RGB distance ≥ 64) checks every pair of states, and each state against construction lines, in both the standard and Windows 95 themes.
  - Fixed colours for selected work planes and shapes were replaced with theme colours.
  - The Windows 95 hover and preview colours now differ in hue, not just brightness.
- **Preview can't be picked or selected.**
  - It lives only in `hover_.preview`: it is not in the scene or the document, and it never becomes a pick candidate.
  - It is cleared on tool change (`DrawingSession::SelectTool` really does discard placed points), on cancel, and after a successful finish.
  - It is kept when finishing fails.
- **Rule 3 (fix from revision 3):** all four Preview draws now use `PreviewPen()`:
  - the drawing-in-progress line
  - the body-drag ghost
  - the control-point drag ghost
  - the fold preview

  The pen uses the Preview width, is dashed, and has alpha 0.75. No fixed-width preview pen remains; the leftover direct `palette_.selected` uses are UI hot-state widgets, not geometry.
- **Rule 1:** Hover does not change the selection, and Selected wins over Hover. Both are tested.
- **Out of scope, and recorded as such in AT-UIX-004:** Input, Warning, Error, Locked and Hidden. Nothing produces those states yet (they wait on ToolSession).
